Privacy Policy

For this Weather Afghanistan website

This policy describes what this static website does. The person or organization that deploys the site operates it; no operator contact details are configured in this project. Add your organization’s contact information before public deployment if users need a privacy contact.

Information handled by this site

  • Location search: a search term is sent to Open-Meteo’s Geocoding API to return matching places. The app does not save the search term.
  • Weather location: coordinates for the selected place are sent to Open-Meteo’s Forecast API. Browser coordinates are used only after you choose “Use my location”. The site does not request location permission on its own.
  • Browser storage: language, theme, selected location, and weather responses are stored in your browser’s local storage. Weather entries are considered fresh for ten minutes; an older saved response may be shown with a stale-data notice if a request fails. The stored entry can remain on the device until it is replaced or you clear site data.

The app has no account system, contact form, advertising, analytics, user-upload feature, or first-party server database. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, or payment information.

Open-Meteo and hosting providers

Weather and location requests go directly from your browser to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. As with any network request, the provider and network operators can receive technical request information such as your IP address; requested coordinates and search terms are included in the API request. This app does not control provider-side logs or retention. Review Open-Meteo’s current terms and privacy information.

The static hosting provider may process connection logs to deliver and secure the site. Its collection and retention depend on the host selected by the site operator; consult that host’s privacy notice.

Retention and choices

App preferences and weather cache are stored on your device. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove them. You can avoid global search and browser location, or stop using the site. Requests already sent to Open-Meteo are governed by the provider’s own policies.

Children and changes

This app is a general weather service and is not designed to identify children. The site operator should review and update this notice if features, hosting, or data flows change.

Weather information is provided for general planning. Do not rely on this site as the sole source for emergency warnings or safety-critical decisions.

Cookie Policy

For this Weather Afghanistan website

This app does not set or read browser cookies for its own operation. It uses browser localStorage, which is different from a cookie and remains on the device until cleared.

Local storage used by the app

  • weather-language remembers the English or Dari selection.
  • weather-theme remembers System, Light, or Dark appearance.
  • weather-location remembers the selected location and coordinates.
  • weather:<coordinates> stores weather results with a timestamp for short-term reuse and stale offline display.

No analytics, advertising, social-media, map, or embedded video cookies are added by this app. Forecast and geocoding calls use HTTPS requests with browser credentials omitted. Open-Meteo and your chosen hosting provider have their own systems and policies; see their current notices for details about their services.

Managing local data

Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove this app’s local storage. Doing so resets the language, theme, selected place, and saved weather. The app does not include a consent banner because it does not set optional tracking cookies; operators should reassess this if they add services or change storage.
